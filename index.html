IELTS Speaking Practice · OshGroup

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy describes how IELTS Speaking Practice, provided by OshGroup (Oybek Atamatov, Uzbekistan), handles information when you use the app. Contact us at oybekatamatov999@gmail.com.

1. App features and information

The app offers IELTS speaking practice and uses online services to support its features. Information handling depends on the features you use.

When you create or use an account, our server stores an account identifier and profile information you provide, such as your name, email, phone number, city, country and profile image. Telegram sign-in also uses your Telegram identifier and associated profile information. We store learning settings, such as study goals and target scores, and practice activity, such as question completion history, scores and points, to provide progress and ranking features. The app also keeps preferences and cached profile information on your device. Connections to our services expose technical information such as your IP address; operational logs may record requests and account or call identifiers for maintenance and troubleshooting.

2. Microphone, recordings and AI processing

Speaking features may require microphone access. You can manage microphone permission in your device settings; disabling it may prevent recording features from working.

Saved practice recordings are stored in the app’s storage on your device and can be deleted from the recorded-answers screen. We do not send recordings or transcripts to an external generative-AI service for scoring. Mock-test scoring is performed in the app. Speech-to-text features use your device’s speech-recognition service, which may send audio to its provider for transcription depending on the device, provider and settings; recognition is not guaranteed to occur entirely offline. During a live practice call, your microphone audio and, if enabled, camera video are transmitted to the other participant. Connection information is exchanged through our server and a Google STUN service to establish the call; this can expose network addresses to connection services and peers. The implemented call feature does not record calls on our server. Other participants may independently capture what you share.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including IP addresses, user interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. IP addresses may be used to estimate a device’s general location. Actual processing depends on SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. You can manage available advertising privacy settings on your Android device.

Advertising settings available through your device or Google account depend on your platform and region. Disabling microphone or camera permissions does not disable advertising-related data processing.

4. How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide the features described above, operate and support the app, and display advertisements. Third-party services process information as described in this policy and their own privacy policies.

When you use the translator, the text you enter and selected languages are sent to Google Translate to obtain a translation. Telegram processes interactions with its sign-in service under its own privacy policy. Leaderboards display your name, profile image where available, points and rank to other users. Live-call participants receive the content you share during a call. Google and Telegram may process information outside your country under their respective policies. See Telegram’s Privacy Policy and the Google policy linked above.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Local recordings remain until you delete them or clear the app’s local storage. Local daily study-time history is pruned to approximately 56 days when new study time is saved. Account and server-side practice records do not have an automatic expiry in the current implementation and remain until manually removed. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing the address below with the subject “IELTS Speaking Practice — data deletion” and enough information to identify your account, such as your registered email or Telegram username. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Requests are handled manually; we will explain any information that cannot be deleted immediately and why. Uninstalling the app does not delete your server account. Third-party providers manage their own retention under their policies.

For questions or requests concerning your information, contact oybekatamatov999@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

6. Security

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

The app connects to our API over HTTPS and uses authenticated access for account features. These safeguards reduce risks but do not eliminate them.

7. Children’s privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about information provided by a child, contact us using the address below so we can review the information and assist with deletion.
